# Review — Indigo calm theme and header/alignment fixes (#276, E19.S1)

## Blocking
### 1. The F2 fix moves the mobile exam submit bar under the floating assistant button
**Where:** `web/src/features/exam/components/ExamRunner.tsx:66` (`sticky bottom-above-tab-bar`), `web/src/features/avatar/components/AvatarDock.tsx:9` (`fixed start-4 bottom-24 z-10 h-12`), utility at `web/src/styles/app.css:119`
**Rule:** plan Goal (“removes the alignment defects (sticky bars hidden under the app bar or tab bar…)”), plan F2; review brief (“the exam submit bar no longer sits under the header or tab bar”).
**Problem:** `AvatarDock` is mounted by the student layout (`routes/student/route.tsx:15`), so it is also shown on `/student/exam/$sessionId`. Below 900 px it is fixed at 96–144 px above the viewport bottom, at z-10. Once stuck, the submit bar now spans 64–134 px above the bottom (64 = `layout.bar` + 8; height 70 = 2 px border + 24 px padding + 44 px button) and has no z-index. The FAB paints over the bar’s top 38 px. Before this change the bar sat at 0–70 px (hidden under the tab bar, which was the old bug) and never met the FAB. So the fix swaps “hidden under the tab bar” for “partly covered by the FAB”. The DOM audit has no overlap check, so `02-layout-audit.md` F2 shows this as “fixed”.
**Failure:** at a 375 px width, ar or en, open a unit exam and scroll so the bar sticks. The «اسأل المساعد» / “Ask the assistant” button (first child, at the start edge of the button group; 77–121 px above the bottom) is about 25 px tall × ~90 px wide under the «المساعد» / “Assistant” pill (start-4, about 120–130 px wide). In en, the two buttons (about 185 + 12 + 126 px) fill the bar’s 317 px inner width, so the FAB lands on the Ask button there too. The covered strip belongs to the FAB, not the bar.
**Fix:** keep the bar clear of the FAB lane on mobile. Smallest options: hide the dock while an exam session is open (the bar already has `AskAvatarButton`), or lift the dock above the stuck bar on that route. Then add the overlap to `02-layout-audit.md` (F2 row) with the measured rects.
